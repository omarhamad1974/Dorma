# Dorma colours and palettes

How to use this file:

1. Each block starts with a line of three dashes: ---
2. In the studio, open Content › Import, choose "Colours and palettes", and pick this file.
   You will see the colours before anything changes.

Two kinds of block:

- My colours: your named colours, one per line as "- Name: #hex". They show as small dots
  next to every colour picker in the studio. A colour already there keeps its place and gets the new name.
- Palette: a saved palette under Design › Colours and type, with four colours:
  Accent, Background, Panels and Text. A palette with the same name is updated.

Everything above the first --- line is ignored.
