# Dorma studio: quick admin manual

The studio is at **/#admin** on the site (for example `https://omarhamad1974.github.io/Dorma/#admin`). Sign in with your editor account.

## The golden rule: Draft, Preview, Publish

- Every change saves to the **draft** on its own ("Draft saved" at the top).
- **Preview draft** shows the draft site. Visitors do not see it.
- **Publish** makes the draft live. Visitors see it right away.
- Made a mistake after publishing? **Site care → Backups** keeps every publish (the last 30 by default). Press **Put in draft** on an older one, check it, then Publish. A safety copy of your current draft is saved first.
- **Site care → Backups → Download backup** saves everything (draft, live site, Social plan, proposals) as one file on your computer. Do it every month and before big changes.

## Where things are

| Tab | Sub-tabs |
|---|---|
| **Outline** | Site outline (also the **Outline** button just before Live preview) |
| **Content** | Pages, Carousels, Blog, Proposals, Import, AI writing, AI archive |
| **Inbox** | Messages, Form settings |
| **Design** | Global style, Colours and type, Logo, Animation, Site and titles |
| **Flows and modals** | Flows, Modals, Icons |
| **Library** | Library, Missing images |
| **Social** | Create posts, Calendar, Social settings |
| **Site care** | Health check, Backups |

Most settings follow a simple chain: **Global style → the item → one slide or one item**. Leave a field empty (or set it to *Global*) to follow the level above. Pick *Custom* to give one thing its own look.

Every "pick a type" choice (section type, ＋ New, background effect, animation, modal type and so on) opens a **visual picker** with a small drawing or a moving preview of each option.

## Finding your way on long screens

Next to the sub-tab names (Pages, Carousels…) there is a small toolbar. It only changes how the studio looks for you, in this browser. Visitors never see it, and the public site is not affected.

- **Groups fold open and closed.** Every box with a title (Page, Sections, Look, Animation…) is a group. Tap its title or the arrow to close or open it. The studio remembers each choice on this browser. Groups most people seldom use (for example *Header on this page*, *Look*, *More button*, *Backup*, *Import…*) start closed.
- **Open all / Close all** open or close every group on the tab (Close all also closes open items such as sections and slides).
- **Find** (or press **/**) lists the groups on the tab so you can jump to one, and searches setting names, items and groups as you type. Pick a result and the studio opens its group, scrolls to it and makes it blink once. Closed items (a section or slide you have not opened yet) are found by their name.
- **Text size:** **A−** and **A+** make all studio text smaller or bigger, from 90% up to 200%. Tap **Aa** to pick a size from the list. It is saved on this browser only.
- **Colour bands:** groups alternate in tone and have a coloured edge (Dorma blue, yellow, magenta, cyan) so you can tell where one ends. Items inside a group alternate too, and the item you have open has a highlighted border.
- **Titles stay in view:** while you scroll through a long group, its title stays at the top of the screen (on computers).
- **Long lists page:** the lists on the left (pages, carousels, flows, modals, blog posts) show 12 at a time with a filter box once there are more than 8. The Library shows 48 pictures per page. **Show all** shows the whole list again.
- **On a phone** the list on the left folds into one row with the item you are editing; tap it to choose another. The toolbar shows Find and Aa (Open all and Close all are inside Find).

## 1. What you can edit

### The whole site at a glance (Outline)
- **Outline** (first in the side menu, or the **Outline** button just before **Live preview** in the top bar) shows every page in menu order, with its sections in the order visitors see them. The line at the top counts pages, pages in the menu, sections and hidden sections.
- Tap a page to open or close it. **Open all** and **Close all** in the toolbar at the top open or close every page at once.
- **Pages:** drag the dots (or use the up and down arrows) to change the order, which is also the menu order. **In menu / Not in menu** switches whether a page shows in the menu. The eye previews the page, the two squares duplicate it (the copy gets its own address and stays out of the menu until you add it), the cross removes it. The home page can't be removed.
- **Sections:** drag to reorder, or drag onto another open page to move it there. **Shown / Hidden** switches it for visitors. Duplicate puts a copy right below. **＋ Add section** under a page opens the visual picker and adds the section at the end of that page.
- **＋ Add page** at the bottom adds a page from a template (Page, Service page, Catalog page, Article page).
- **Jump to design** (or tap a section's name) opens that page in Content → Pages with the section open and in view. Hero carousels open in Content → Carousels.
- Removing asks first, and says if any buttons link to that page or section. After any change in Outline, an **Undo** button appears at the top. Undo goes back one step at a time, and only while you haven't changed the same pages somewhere else in the studio.
- **Not live yet** marks pages and sections that are in the draft but not published.

### Pages and sections (Content → Pages)
- Add, delete and rename pages, change their address, choose a layout (Cinematic, Index, Console) and show or hide them in the menu.
- Each page is a stack of **sections**. Add a section from the visual picker, drag to reorder, **hide** (stays saved, not shown), or **duplicate**.
- Section types: Hero, Hero carousel, Chapters, Numbers, About, Clients, Card grid, Accordion, Timeline, Flow strip, Wide banner, Division, Call to action, Feature list, Testimonials, Quote request form, Catalog, Blog posts, Text article, Photo only, Logo.
- Inside each section: all text, images, buttons and what buttons do (open a modal, go to a page, open a post or article, go to a slide, open a link).
- **More options** on a section: a label (so any button can jump to it), a flow under the section, a background effect, its animation, and its own look (spacing, width, background, title font and colours).
- **Header on this page** lets one page have its own header colours.

### Icons, hiding parts, logo and button (Content → Pages → section → Icon, text, logo and button)
Every section has a folding box **Icon, text, logo and button**, right under its own settings:
- **Icon by the title**: pick any icon (icons are added under Flows and modals → Icons), then **Before the title**, **After the title** or **Above the title**, its size (1 = as tall as the title), colour, and animation (Pop in, Spin in, Draw its lines, Rise, Float, Pulse, Glow, Swing). On Arabic pages "before" is on the right, as you would expect.
- **Cards, accordion rows, chapters, linked items, catalog items and slides** each have their own **Icon by the title** and **Icon position** (open the item).
- **Hide parts**: Hide the subtitle (eyebrow and small labels), Hide the title, Hide the text. Cards and rows have **Hide the subtitle** (code and tag, tags, caption, type and price line, role) and **Hide the text**. Slides have Hide the eyebrow and Hide the short paragraph. Hidden text stays saved, so switching it off brings it back.
- **Logo in this section**: show any logo version (automatic, white, colour, silver or an added version) above or below the section's content, with its height (and a phone height), alignment, entrance (Fade, Rise, Pop, Blur, Spin, Drop, Wipe, Flip), a movement that keeps going (Float, Pulse, Glow, Light sweeps across, Breathe, Nudge), a delay, and an optional click action.
- **Button (call to action)**: a button with an optional line above it, size (Small to Extra large), style (Site button, Solid accent, Outline, Glowing ring, Text with an arrow), place, alignment, entrance, movement and delay.
- **Heading style and cards**: the heading's style (Gradient, Shimmer, Glow, Outline letters, Marker highlight, Underline draws in, CMYK print offset), how it arrives (Letter by letter, Word by word, Blur, Typewriter, Fade up) and what cards do under the pointer (Lift, Glow, 3D tilt, Picture zooms, Accent border, Light sweeps across).
- Carousels have the same icon, logo, button and title style under **Content → Carousels → Icon, logo, button and title style**.
- The defaults for the whole site are under **Design → Global style → Headings, icons and cards**.

### Photo only and Logo sections
- **Photo only**: one picture from the Library with a **mask** (Rounded, Pill, Arch, Circle, Hexagon, Diamond, Slanted bottom, Cut corners, Leaf, Soft blob, Frame, Fade out at the bottom/top/sides, Soft oval, Wave at the bottom, Diagonal stripes, Halftone dots, or **Inside a word**, where the photo shows through big letters such as DORMA), **opacity** (1 = solid, lower = see-through, with an optional colour behind it), height (21:9 to portrait, or full screen), width (edge to edge, page, narrow, small) and alignment, Fill or Show the whole photo, blur, black and white, slow zoom as you scroll, movement with the scroll, its own colour filter (or as set in the Library) and an optional caption.
- **Logo**: the logo on its own, any version, any height, aligned start, centre or end, with an entrance, a movement that keeps going and an optional click action.
- **Call to action** now also has a short line under the heading, button size, style, alignment, entrance and movement.

### Carousels and slides (Content → Carousels)
- All hero carousels in one place. Add, reorder, duplicate, hide slides.
- Each slide: picture, eyebrow, title, paragraph, its own flow, its own colours and filter, and its own timing.
- **Seconds on screen has no maximum**, so a slide can act like a page. Or turn on **Stay on this slide until the visitor moves on**.
- **Move to next slide automatically** can be turned off per carousel.

### Blog (Content → Blog)
- Write posts with simple formatting (`## Heading`, `- list`, `**bold**`, links), insert pictures from the Library, add a cover, tags and summary. Untick **Published** to keep a post as a draft.

### Client proposals (Content → Proposals)
A proposal is a small website made for one client, showing only the services they need, in the site's look. The client gets a link like `…/#for/acme-k7p2`.
- **New proposal**, then enter the client's name (English and Arabic) and pick their **logo** from the Library (upload it first under Library, Brand & Identity). Choose a white tile behind colour or dark logos, or none for white logos. Without a logo, the name is shown.
- **Services:** tick the pages to include. Each one becomes a page of the proposal with the same sections, modals and flows as the main site. Reorder with the arrows, write a **note for this client** to replace the card text on the overview, and untick **sections** the client doesn't need.
- **Overview page** (made for you): Dorma and client logos, the date, "Prepared for …", your headline and **welcome message**, a card per service, **related work** (Library pictures and published blog posts you pick) and the **contact button**. The words around it ("Prepared for", "Services in this proposal", the full website link) can be changed per proposal.
- **Header:** a band with the client's logo, "Prepared for …", the date and **View the full website**; a menu with only the proposal's pages; the language switch. Buttons to pages outside the proposal open the full website in a new tab.
- **Link and status:** **Copy link**, **Open**, or **Preview with draft** (shows unpublished studio changes). **Live** = the link works; **Off** = the client sees "This proposal is no longer available"; **Archived** = off and moved to the Archived list to reuse later. **Expires on** stops the link the day after that date. **Search engines may list it**: untick to add a noindex tag (links with `#` are rarely listed anyway).
- **Opens:** how many times the client opened it and when (phone or computer, English or Arabic). Your own opens while signed in are not counted. **Reset count** clears them.
- **New opens:** when a client opens a proposal, a yellow number appears on **Content** and on the **Proposals** sub-tab, and the list shows "· 2 new". Open that proposal in the studio and the new opens count as seen. The studio checks every two minutes while it's open. (No email alert: that would need a paid or extra email service.)
- **Version: Live or Frozen.** **Live** (the default) follows the published site: improve a service page, publish, and the proposal shows it. **Freeze this version** keeps a copy of the service pages, pictures, modals and flows the proposal uses, as they are published now, so later site edits don't change what the client agreed to. The badge next to **Version** (and "frozen" in the list) shows which mode each proposal is in. **Update the frozen copy** takes a fresh copy; **Go back to live** follows the site again. The welcome, client details and words stay editable while frozen; colours, fonts and the logo always follow the site. A service ticked after freezing shows live until you update the copy (the studio warns you).
- **PDF:** **PDF (English)** / **PDF (Arabic)** open a clean white, A4 version with both logos, "Prepared for …", the date, the welcome, a list of the services, each service's picture and text, related work and how to reach Dorma (the email under Site and titles). Press **Save as PDF** and choose *Save as PDF* in the print window. Forms, buttons and animations are left out. The client also gets a **Save as PDF** link in the proposal's header (switch it off under *Languages, notes and copies*). The PDF link is `…/#for/<code>/pdf/en` or `/pdf/ar`.
- **Request a Quote** on a proposal sends the message to **Inbox**, marked *Proposal* with the client's name and a link back to the proposal.
- **Languages:** same as the site, English only, Arabic only, or both with a switch, and which language it opens in.
- **Duplicate for another client** copies the services, texts and work, clears the client and switches the copy off so you can tune it first.
- Proposals save as soon as you change them (no Publish needed) and are kept in their own database table, never in the public site settings, so nobody can list your clients. The **service pages they show follow the published site**: improve a service page and publish, and every proposal shows the update.

### Inbox (Inbox)
Messages people send from the site's forms: the **Contact form** section, the **Quote request form** on service pages and catalogs, the **Request a Quote** modal, and Request a Quote on client proposals.
- **Messages:** unread messages are bold with a yellow dot, and their number shows on the **Inbox** tab. Click a message to read it (it becomes *Read*). **Reply by email** opens your email app with a reply ready (in Arabic if they wrote in Arabic). **Copy**, **Mark unread**, **Archive** (moves it to *Archived*) and **Delete** (for good).
- **Views and search:** Inbox, Unread, Archived and All; filter by type (Contact, Quote requests, From proposals) and search names, emails, companies and message text. Tick several messages to mark, archive or delete them together.
- **Export CSV** downloads the messages shown (or the ticked ones) as a spreadsheet file that opens in Excel with Arabic intact.
- **Form settings:** where messages go: **Studio Inbox** (recommended) or **the visitor's email app** (nothing is saved). The **thank-you message** visitors see after sending, in English and Arabic. **Dorma's email** is used when the Inbox can't be reached and on printable proposals. Publish to apply.
- **Spam protection** (free, nothing to set up): a hidden field that bots fill in, a minimum of 2.5 seconds to fill the form, and limits of 5 messages per visitor in 10 minutes, 5 per email address an hour and 300 a day for the whole site. The Inbox keeps the newest 5,000 messages.
- **Privacy:** only signed-in editors can read messages. Visitors can send a message but can never read, list or change any.
- **Add a contact form:** Pages → Add section → **Contact form** (name, email, phone, company and message). A **Quote request form** can also be switched to a contact message with its **Form** field.
- **Email alerts are not set up yet.** New messages show in the studio only. A free email alert is possible with a free Resend account; ask for it as a new task.

### Import (Content → Import)
- Bring in pages, sections, slides, flows, modals, posts or library names from a Markdown file, with a preview first. **Download template** gives a filled-in example.

### Write with AI (Content → AI writing)
- A small **AI** button sits in the corner of the text boxes all over the studio (titles, paragraphs, lists, blog posts, buttons, Library alt text and SEO). It opens a window where you can:
  - **Write new**: say in a few words what it should say, and get English and Arabic side by side.
  - **Improve** or **Shorten** the text that is there.
  - **Translate** English to Arabic or Arabic to English.
  - **Describe the picture** (Library → an image → SEO): alt text, title, caption and description written from the picture itself.
- Nothing changes until you press **Use this**. You can edit the suggestion first, and untick a language to keep what you had. Then it saves like any other edit, and visitors only see it after **Publish**.
- The AI uses only real facts: your notes under **What the AI should know**, the box itself and the boxes around it. It never makes up clients, projects, numbers or results; where a fact is missing it writes **[To be supplied by Dorma]** (Arabic: [تُضاف من دورما]) for you to fill in. Always read it before you publish.
- **Use general industry knowledge** (a tick box in the AI window, on by default; change the default under AI writing): the AI may also explain general topics that aren't on the site, such as how offset and digital printing differ, paper and finishes, or how a booth goes up. Facts about Dorma itself (clients, results, numbers, and which services or machines Dorma offers) still come only from your site and notes. Untick it when the text must stick strictly to what the site says.
- **Style and references** (fold-out in the AI window): pick a **preset** (Social media post, Service page, Technical guide, or your own) or set each choice for this text: **Length** (fits the box, short, medium, long, or an exact number of words), **Tone**, **Purpose** (marketing, informative, technical, storytelling, social media post, search engines), **Formality**, **Reading level**, **Sentences**, **Arabic style** (formal, modern marketing, light Gulf flavour) and **Call to action**, plus **Audience** and **Extra instructions**. **Save as preset** keeps the current choices.
- **Reference websites**: tick saved websites or paste a link just for this time (up to 3 per request). The AI reads those pages for facts and the right terms, writes in its own words, and never presents another company's clients or numbers as Dorma's. Under the result it shows which pages it read and which it couldn't (pages that need a login or JavaScript can't be read).
- **Save to archive** keeps any AI text with its brief and style. **Content → AI archive** lists them with search and tags: edit or copy the text, **Add to Social calendar** (a drafted post on the platform and date you pick), or delete it. **Write something new with AI** there writes text that isn't tied to a box (for example a LinkedIn post) straight into the archive. To use an archived text in a box, open the box's AI window and choose **From archive**.
- **AI writing** tab: the status (Ready, or what is missing), the AI buttons on or off, general industry knowledge on or off by default, the model, the **Default style**, **Saved styles (presets)**, **Reference websites** (**Flash** for better writing, **Flash-Lite** for more free uses), the brand notes, Arabic notes, words to avoid, and a **Try it** box.
- It uses Google's free **Gemini** service through your Supabase project. The key is kept in Supabase, never on the site or in GitHub. Each editor can make 150 AI requests a day (and 8 a minute); Google's free allowance also has limits, and the studio tells you in plain words when one is reached.
- **One-time setup** (also shown step by step in the AI writing tab):
  1. Open **Google AI Studio → API keys** (aistudio.google.com/app/apikey), sign in with your Google account, press **Create API key** and copy it. No billing needed; leave billing off to stay free.
  2. Open **Supabase → Edge Functions → Secrets** for the Dorma project, add a secret named `GEMINI_API_KEY` with the key as its value, and save.
  3. In the studio, press **Check again** under Content → AI writing. It should say **Ready**.
- On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its services, so don't paste private or confidential information into the AI window.

### Flows, modals and icons (Flows and modals)
- **Flows**: the row of buttons or items under a title. Add items (Button, Text, Icon, Animated note, Custom SVG), give each an action, and choose how the flow behaves: **Row**, **Tabs** or **Reveal**.
- **Modals**: the pop-up panels. Five types: Media, Gallery, Flow, Service list, and Sections (any page sections inside a modal). Edit text, pictures, flow, look, animation and the More button.
- **Icons**: add icons and edit their SVG.

### Design (Design)
- **Global style**: the look for everything at once: text and accent colours, header, menu, image filter, every flow, every carousel and slide, every section, every modal, light and dark mode. Save it as a named **saved style** and switch between styles later.
- **Colours and type**: palettes, My colours, background colours, fonts and sizes for titles, subtitles, body, labels, Arabic and handwriting.
- **Logo**: white, colour and silver logo, your own SVG versions, and the size of the logo in the header, menu, footer and loader.
- **Animation**: overall speed, easing, title reveal, sections on scroll, number count-up, mouse parallax, button glow, modal opening and closing.
- **Site and titles**: brand name, footer, home page, header button, forms and enquiries (where form messages go, the thank-you message and Dorma's email), languages (English, Arabic or both) and numbering, the page loader, page titles, and copying or pasting all settings as text (backup files and versions are under Site care).

### Library (Library)
- All pictures, videos, articles and generated scenes, sorted into **categories** (a category is required).
- Per item: name, tags, alt text, focus point, colour overlay, hide from library.
- **Quick upload** stores files in Supabase. **Picture optimizing** shrinks photos first (resized, saved as WebP, PNG kept for transparent logos). Tick **Keep originals** to skip it once.
- **Missing images** lists every empty slot, placeholder or broken picture on the site with a **Go there** button.
- Image pickers show **pictures not used on the site yet** first.

### Social (Social)
- **Create posts**: pick any text from the site (pages, sections, blog posts, catalog items, flows) and the studio writes ready-to-copy posts for **LinkedIn, Facebook Page and Instagram**, in English and Arabic when the site has Arabic. It suggests a picture, crops it to each platform's size and saves it as a JPG, and can make carousel slides. Press **Add to calendar** to plan it.
- **Calendar**: your posting plan. **Fill empty rhythm days** adds suggestions from the site, or **Suggest a post**. Mark each post drafted, scheduled or posted, and add the link to the live post.
- **Social settings**: platforms on or off, tone and template per platform, call to action, hashtags, carousel slides, the weekly rhythm, and the site address that posts link back to (change it when the site gets its own domain). Optional tracking on links.
- The plan is saved in the studio database for editors only. It is never published on the site.

### Site care (Site care)
**Health check** lists what needs attention on the site, checked in the draft. The tiles at the top count **Broken** (visitors hit a problem), **To fix** and **Good to know**; tap a tile to see only that level. Each row says where the problem is, and **Fix it** opens that page, section, carousel, modal, flow, blog post or library item. **Ignore** hides a row you're happy with (it stays hidden, and **Show ignored** brings it back). The checks:
- **Broken links and buttons**: buttons that go to a page, modal, blog post or article that was removed, a section label that isn't there any more, an unpublished blog post, an empty or wrong web address, and flows that were deleted.
- **Missing pictures**: pictures removed from the library, and how many places have no picture or a placeholder (opens Missing images).
- **Alt text**: pictures used on the site with no description.
- **Arabic text**: when the site has Arabic, every section, modal, flow and published post whose texts have no Arabic yet, grouped with the field names. Alt text without Arabic is listed here too.
- **Pages nobody can reach**: pages not in the menu with no button going to them, and pages with nothing to show.
- **Large files**: pictures over 1.5 MB that are used on the site.
- **Settings**: for example forms set to the email app with no email set.
- **Hidden things**: hidden sections and slides, and blog posts that aren't published.
- **Unused library items**: pictures and videos not used anywhere.
- **Outside links**: press **Check outside links** to try every outside web address. A browser can only tell whether the address answers, so open a link to be sure.

Hidden sections and draft posts are left out of the other checks.

At the top, **Site connection and backups** shows:
- **Keep-alive**: the free Supabase plan pauses a project after about a week without use. A GitHub job pings the database every day at 05:23 UTC and checks that the site answers. *Awake* means the last ping came in the last two days. If pings stop, press **Open on GitHub**: GitHub turns scheduled jobs off after 60 days without changes to the repository, and **Enable workflow** there turns it back on. **Ping now** wakes it by hand.
- **Database size**: how much of the free 500 MB is used, and how much the saved versions take (about 75 KB each).
- **Backup file**: when a backup file was last downloaded on this computer.

**Backups**
- **Download backup**: one file with the draft, the live site, the Social plan and the client proposals. Pictures and videos are not in the file; they stay in the Library and the file keeps their names.
- **Bring back from a file**: choose a backup file (or a downloaded version). The studio shows what's in it, then **Put it in the draft** replaces the draft, after saving a safety copy of the current draft. You can also replace the Social plan, and add back proposals that were deleted (existing proposals are never changed). Check with Preview draft, then Publish.
- **Version history**: every Publish, every **Save a snapshot now** (give it a name, for example *Before the new home page*) and every safety copy. For each version: a name, **Pin** (a pinned version is never removed), **Compare with the draft** (what changed since), **Download**, **Put in draft** and **Delete**. The version visitors see now can't be deleted.
- **Versions to keep**: 5 to 100 (30 by default). Older unpinned versions are removed when a new one is saved.

## 2. What you cannot edit in the studio

These need a code change or a change in GitHub or Supabase. Ask for them as a new task.

- **New kinds of things**: new section types, new background effects, new animations, new modal types, new flow behaviours, new fonts beyond the lists offered.
- **The studio itself**: its tabs, fields and layout.
- **Who can edit**: editor accounts are added in Supabase, not in the studio.
- **Library files on GitHub**: adding files to the `public/library/` folders, creating a new category folder, and deleting files is done on GitHub (the studio opens the right GitHub page for you). The site redeploys in about two minutes, then press **Check GitHub for new files**. GitHub takes up to 25 MB per file.
- **Email alerts** for new messages or proposal opens: they need an email service (for example a free Resend account) and a small server function. Until then, check the Inbox and Proposals badges in the studio.
- **Translation**: the studio's **AI** button can draft the Arabic (or the English) for you, but it is a suggestion: read it, fix it, and press **Use this** only when it's right.
- **The Gemini key and AI limits**: the key is a secret in Supabase (Edge Functions → Secrets), and the daily limit per editor is a Supabase setting (`AI_DAILY_LIMIT`), not a studio field.
- **The real logo SVG**: upload it under Design → Logo once you have it. Until then the PNG is used, and the loader's **Draw** animation needs an SVG.
- **Hosting, domain and Supabase plan**: the site runs on GitHub Pages and the free Supabase plan. The daily keep-alive stops Supabase from pausing; if it ever does pause (for example the GitHub job was off for a week), restore it in the Supabase dashboard.
- **Posting to social media**: the Social tab writes posts and pictures for you, but it does not post them. You copy the text, save the picture and post it yourself on LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram.

## 3. Animation and effects, part by part

**Where to set it once for everything:** Design → Global style and Design → Animation.
**Where to change one thing:** open that item and pick *Custom*, or change a field that says *Global*.
Always check with **Preview draft** (most panels also have a **Replay** or **Preview** button).

### Timing (who appears when)
- Timing fields are in **seconds from when the slide, section or modal starts**. Lower numbers come first. Empty uses the default shown in grey.
- Found on: Hero, Hero carousel and each slide, Timeline, Flow strip, Division, and Modals.
- Carousel default: title at 0s, paragraph at 0.5s, flow at 1.5s, flow items 0.2s apart (**Gap between flow items**).
- Order of priority for slides: **slide → carousel → Global style → Every carousel and slide**.

### Hero carousel slides
- **Design → Global style → Every carousel and slide**: slide transition (Light wipe, Crossfade, Slide, Zoom through), seconds per slide (any length), slow cinematic zoom (amount and duration), the carousel image filter and slide colours.
- **Content → Carousels**: each carousel and each slide can override timing, colours and filter. **Replay the flow animation on every slide** replays it even when slides share a flow.
- Flows never move slides: each slide tells its own story.

### Titles
- **Design → Animation → Title reveal**: Letter by letter, Word by word, Fade up, Blur in.

### Flows (rows of buttons and items)
- **Design → Global style → Every flow**: animation for all flows.
- **Flows and modals → Flows → Animation and timing**: animate the whole flow at once or each item in turn, animation (Rise, Fade, Scale, Blur, Slide, Flip, Pop, None), duration, gap between items, start delay.
- Each flow item can **Animate this item on its own** with its own animation, duration and extra delay.
- Buttons glow to invite clicks: **Design → Animation → Button glow** (0 turns it off).

### Sections (on scroll)
- **Design → Global style → Section entrance and exit** sets it for all sections.
- One section: **Pages → section → More options → Animation**.
- **Entrance**: Rise, Fade, Scale up, Zoom in, Blur, Slide left or right, Tilt, Skew, Wipe, Circle opens, Open from the centre, Each part in turn.
- **Exit** (as it scrolls away): Fade, Shrink, Blur, Parallax, Darken, Fold back, Slide away.
- Set the entrance length, and **Replay entrance** to play it each time it scrolls into view.
- **Design → Animation → Sections on scroll** and **Count numbers up** are the simple site-wide switches.

### Headings, icons, logo and buttons
- **Design → Global style → Headings, icons and cards** for every section; one section under **Icon, text, logo and button** (see part 1).
- **Heading style**: Gradient, Shimmer (light runs through), Glow, Outline letters, Marker highlight (draws in), Underline draws in, CMYK print offset.
- **Heading entrance**: Letter by letter, Word by word, Blur in, Typewriter, Fade up (it plays when the heading scrolls into view).
- **Icons by titles**: Pop in, Spin in, Draw its lines, Rise, or keep moving: Float, Pulse, Glow, Swing.
- **Logo and button blocks**: entrance (Fade, Rise, Pop, Blur, Spin, Drop, Wipe, Flip) and a movement that keeps going (Float, Pulse, Glow, Light sweeps across, Breathe, Nudge now and then), each with a delay.
- **Card hover**: Lift, Glow, 3D tilt, Picture zooms, Accent border, Light sweeps across.
- Visitors who prefer reduced motion see all of these at rest.

### Pictures: reveals, masks and hover
- **Design → Global style → Pictures: masks, reveals and hover**, or per section under **More options → Animation**.
- **Reveal as it scrolls in**: Wipe, Circle, Diamond, Diagonal, Split, Opens from a line, Curtain, Blinds, Shutters, Tiles, CMYK print pass, Soft spotlight, Zoom out of a frame, Blur. Choose the direction, length and cover colour.
- **Shape (mask kept on the picture)**: Rounded, Pill, Arch, Circle, Hexagon, Diamond, Slanted bottom, Cut corners, Leaf, Soft blob.
- **When the pointer is over it**: Slow zoom, 3D tilt, Light follows the pointer, Black and white to colour, Lift with a glow.

### Colour filters on pictures
- **Design → Global style → Image filter**: a colour or gradient over every picture, its strength, dark shade on heroes, brightness and film grain.
- Override per Library item (**Colour overlay**), per carousel or per slide.

### Background effects (animated, behind a section)
- Global: **Design → Global style → Every section → Background effect**. One section: **More options → Background effect**.
- 26 effects, for example Contour lines, Orbits, Spotlight grid, Liquid colour, CMYK stripes, Waves, Halftone, Aurora, Ink in water, Print registration marks, Starfield, Ripples, Floating shapes. The picker plays each one.
- Settings: colours (one, two, or Dorma CMYK), strength, speed, amount, size, direction, where it gathers, reacts to the mouse, and a veil so text stays readable.
- They only run while the section is on screen.

### Modals (pop-ups)
- **Design → Global style → Every modal**, or **Design → Animation → Modals**: opening and closing for all modals.
- One modal: **Flows and modals → Modals → Animation**.
- **Opening**: Scan line, Fade, Scale up, Slide up, Circle from the click, Grow from the click, Wipe, Curtain, Unfold, Diagonal, Flip down, Drop and bounce, Blur, Colour bars, CMYK print pass.
- **Closing**: Match the opening, or Fade, Shrink, Slide down, Circle into the click, and more.
- Look: width, panel colour and strength, blur and page brightness behind, corner marks, scan lines. Modals also have their own **timing**.

### Menu
- **Design → Global style → Header** and **Menu**.
- Header link style (Underline, Line above, Dot, Pill, Brackets, Glow) and hover (Brighten, Line grows, Lift, Glow).
- Full-screen menu **Opening** (Fade, Slide in, Drop, Circle from the button, Curtain, Wipe down, CMYK print pass), how **links arrive** (Rise, Fade, Slide, Grow, Blur, All at once), an animated background, logo and a line at the bottom.
- Tip: preview with a narrow window, or set the menu button to show on every screen.

### Logo and loader
- **Design → Logo → Silver logo**: finish, gradient angle and **Shine** (one sweep, or now and then).
- **Design → Site and titles → Loader**: Reveal, Draw (needs an SVG logo) or Pulse; exit by wipe or fade; minimum time on screen.

### Light and dark mode
- **Design → Global style → Light and dark mode**: what visitors start in (their device, dark or light), show the sun and moon switch, and the light mode colours. Heroes and carousels stay dark.

### Overall feel
- **Design → Animation → Overall speed** speeds up or slows down everything; **Easing** sets the character (sharp, smooth, gentle, overshoot, springy). **Mouse parallax on heroes** adds depth.
